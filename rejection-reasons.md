# Rejection reasons — decoded and fixed

Google's rejection emails use a few standard phrases. Here is what each
one actually means and the fix that works. After fixing, let the changes
publish and index (at least a few days of real improvement) before
re-applying.

## "Low value content"

The most common rejection. It means the reviewer found pages that give a
visitor nothing original or useful.

Usual causes → fixes:

- Thin posts (a few lines, an embed, an image) → expand into complete
  articles or merge several thin posts into one strong one
- Copied or lightly rewritten content → rewrite from scratch in your own
  words, or remove
- Posts copied from official notices/ads with no added explanation →
  add original guidance: who can apply, how, what to prepare
- Auto-generated or spun text → delete; write fewer, real posts
- Many posts, all on the same micro-topic saying the same thing →
  consolidate

## "Site not ready" / "Your site isn't ready to show ads yet"

A bucket reason covering site-quality problems:

- Site looks unfinished (default theme text, sample posts, empty pages)
  → finish or remove everything default
- Navigation broken or confusing → rebuild the menu; test every link
- Site very new with little content → publish steadily for a few weeks,
  then apply
- Pages not indexed → check Search Console coverage, fix robots/sitemap

## "Policy violations found" / specific policy named

Take the named policy literally:

- **Prohibited content** → remove the content entirely (not just from
  the menu — delete or fully rewrite the post)
- **Copyrighted content** → remove the copied text/media; do not re-apply
  while any of it remains
- **Deceptive content** (fake download buttons, misleading claims) →
  remove the deception, keep the honest page

## "Multiple accounts" / account already exists

The person already has an AdSense account (possibly an old, forgotten
one). Fix: recover and use that account. Creating another is a ban risk,
not a shortcut.

## "Site unavailable" / "Couldn't verify your site"

- Verification code missing, removed, or on the wrong site → re-add it
  exactly as instructed and leave it in place
- Site was down, or blocked by a firewall/CDN during review → fix
  hosting, then re-apply
- Applied with a different URL than the live one (www vs non-www,
  http vs https) → apply with the exact canonical URL

## What never works

- Re-applying the same day with nothing changed
- Applying again and again weekly (can slow future reviews)
- Buying an "approved" account (banned on first payout review, and the
  money is lost)
- Hiding bad pages from the reviewer with redirects or cloaking
