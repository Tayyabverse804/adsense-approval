# Pre-application audit checklist

Every box must pass before applying. Report failures with the page/post
named — "some content is thin" is not an audit finding.

## Content

- [ ] At least 15–20 complete, published posts (more for competitive
      niches like finance/health)
- [ ] Every post is original — written for this site, not copied, spun,
      translated-and-pasted, or lightly rewritten from another site
- [ ] Posts are complete articles (a real answer, not 2–3 lines, a video
      embed alone, or an image with a caption)
- [ ] No empty categories, "coming soon" pages, or under-construction
      sections reachable from the menu
- [ ] No posts that are only lists of links, downloads, or job/form
      notices copied word-for-word from an official ad without any
      original explanation around them
- [ ] Images are the site's own, or free-to-use with the source kept
      (no Google Images grabs of copyrighted photos)
- [ ] Contact details and claims on the site are real and consistent

## Mandatory pages (published, linked in menu or footer)

- [ ] **About** — who runs the site, what it publishes, why
- [ ] **Contact** — a working way to reach the owner (form or email)
- [ ] **Privacy Policy** — includes the AdSense/third-party cookie
      disclosure (see `templates/privacy-policy.md`)
- [ ] **Disclaimer** — for jobs, finance, health or download niches

## Navigation & usability

- [ ] Menu works on mobile; every menu link opens a real page
- [ ] Categories/labels each contain real posts
- [ ] Search works; 404 page exists and looks finished
- [ ] No popups, popunders, or auto-redirects from other ad networks
      during the review period
- [ ] Site loads acceptably on a phone connection

## Technical

- [ ] Custom domain connected and loading over HTTPS (strongly
      recommended; free subdomains review worse and some features are
      limited)
- [ ] Blogger: correct blog timezone and language set; comments and
      pages display normally
- [ ] WordPress: no "Hello world" post, sample page, or default tagline
      left anywhere
- [ ] Site is indexed: `site:<domain>` in Google shows the real pages
- [ ] No malware warnings, mixed-content errors, or expired-certificate
      problems

## Policy safety (full list in policy-rules.md)

- [ ] No prohibited content anywhere — including old posts and pages
- [ ] No copied lyrics, book texts, exam papers with answers sold as
      "leaks", or pirated downloads
- [ ] Ads from other networks, if any, do not mimic AdSense or overlap
      content deceptively

**If any box fails: fix, re-audit, then apply.**
