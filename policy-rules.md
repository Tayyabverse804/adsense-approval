# AdSense policy rules in plain words

These are Google's published rules, simplified. Breaking them risks
warnings, ad limits, or a permanent account ban — and a ban is for the
person, not just the site.

## Invalid activity (the account-killers)

- Never click your own ads — not once, not "to test"
- Never ask anyone to click ads ("support my site by clicking")
- Never pay for clicks, join click-exchange groups, or use bots,
  auto-refresh, or traffic-exchange sites
- Never place ads so visitors click by accident: overlapping buttons,
  ads disguised as menus/download buttons, ads that shift content
  while the page loads
- If invalid-traffic warnings arrive: find and stop the traffic source,
  don't ignore the email

## Content that can never carry AdSense

- Adult/sexual content, graphic violence, hate or harassment content
- Pirated content: movies, software, books, lyrics sites built on
  copied text, "leaked" exam papers
- Hacking/cracking tutorials, cheat tools, drugs, weapons sales
- Fake documents, IDs, certificates or results services
- Misleading content: fake news presented as real, deceptive claims,
  fake download/play buttons
- Copied content generally — even "allowed" topics fail if the text
  isn't yours

## Placement rules

- Ads must be clearly ads — never label them as "resources", "links",
  or navigation
- No ads in popups, popunders, emails, or software
- No ads on "thank you", error, login, or under-construction pages
- Don't modify the ad code
- Other ad networks are allowed alongside AdSense only if their ads
  don't mimic AdSense or break the placement rules above

## Account rules

- One AdSense account per person, for life — a second account is a
  violation even if the first is old or unused
- You must be 18+; Google's stated route for younger publishers is a
  parent/guardian's account in their name
- Identity, address (PIN verification) and tax/bank details must be
  real and the account holder's own
- Never sell, rent, or transfer an AdSense account

## ads.txt

- When AdSense gives you an ads.txt line, publish it exactly — it
  proves your inventory is authorized and protects revenue
- Blogger: Settings → Monetization → Custom ads.txt
- WordPress: a file named `ads.txt` in the domain root (or a plugin)
